# Benchmark comparison hierarchy — 28 October 2026

User feedback: the photo dots look nearly equal, so Dietfit does not stand out.

## Assessment

Two independent reviews agreed. On the 174px, 0–100 plot, 82.0% is only 3.44px above 80.0%. That is mathematically correct; the missing piece is interpretive hierarchy. The narrow Impeccable 4.6.0 CLI scan found no deterministic issues. Its browser overlay was unavailable because PRODUCT.md and DESIGN.md are absent; a separate isolated browser page verified actual layout and keyboard behavior.

The design review scored the existing chart 28/40 across ten heuristics: status 3, real-world match 3, control 3, consistency 3, prevention 3, recognition 2, efficiency 3, minimalism 2, recovery 3, help 3. Two of eight cognitive-load checks failed (hierarchy and focal point). These are qualitative design ratings, not a product benchmark.

Priority issues: no immediate takeaway; identical score styling; a large sparse plot with little interpretation. New visitors infer equality from the dots. Skeptical readers need the sample scope and precise margin. Mobile readers should see all three photo results together.

## Implemented

- Highlight the Dietfit column with a warm surface, stronger score and Highest average label (#1 on narrow photo charts).
- Show the +2.0 percentage-point lead over Cal AI, derived from the recorded data. Copy says “Dietfit leads this test,” not a universal or statistically significant superiority claim.
- Keep competitor logos in their original colors and their scores legible.
- Fit all three photo apps without horizontal scrolling; retain the category plot's scrolling and tooltips.
- Replace the photo tooltip instruction with sample/method/scale context. Tooltip behavior remains available.
