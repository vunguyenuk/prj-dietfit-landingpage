# Benchmark demo sticker direction

Updated 28 October 2026 following the user’s request for more expressive, current stickers.

The sticker pack is original, editable inline SVG and HTML in `content/components/benchmark-scan-showcase.html`; presentation and responsive placement are in `styles/dietfit-benchmark-showcase.css`.

- Dietfit orange #FE692E, peach #FFD2BD, warm amber and charcoal.
- Scalloped food seal, camera ticket, smiling burst, little-wins burst and filled label stickers, with cream vinyl edges.
- The salad mascot reuses the existing, unchanged official 2D Figma export `assets/brand/mascots/eating-salad.png`. Its provenance is recorded alongside that asset in `source.json`. No artwork from another designer is copied or downloaded.
- Preserve the reference layout and video. Demo stage corners match other Benchmark sections: 32px desktop, 24px mobile.
- Decorative stickers are hidden from assistive technology and have no pointer or animation behavior.

Visual research (inspiration, not source artwork):
- [FRIDAY — Fast Food Branding & Packaging, Hira Ashraf, 2026](https://www.behance.net/gallery/248552827/Friday-Fast-Food-Branding-Packaging): confident, playful food-brand personality.
- [VistaPrint — Design that sticks: A practical guide to custom micro-merch, 2026](https://www.vistaprint.com/hub/designing-micro-merch/): distinctive die-cut silhouettes and readable brand graphics.
