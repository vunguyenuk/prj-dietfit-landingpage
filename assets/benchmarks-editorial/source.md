# Benchmarks editorial food photograph

- Tool: built-in `image_gen`.
- Final asset: `assets/benchmarks-editorial/food-table.webp`.
- Usage: photographic opening and closing of the English benchmark report. The phone, video, scores and benchmark results remain real HTML/CSS and are not baked into the photograph.

## Final prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: wide landscape photographic background for Dietfit's nutrition benchmark landing page, placed behind an actual editable smartphone demo.
Primary request: create a sophisticated sunny editorial still life of fresh colorful food framing an open central area. A pale warm ivory kitchen table photographed directly from above, with a loose abundant arrangement of market vegetables, fruits and a single prepared grain salad at the far edges: ripe red tomatoes on the vine, cut orange carrots, bright lemons, a halved avocado, lush leafy herbs, a few radishes, green peas and an ivory bowl with a colorful grain salad partly cropped at the lower left. Food has appealing real texture, tiny imperfections and crisp natural detail. No people.
Composition/framing: extra-wide landscape 3:2 image, most food along the left and right outer quarters and cropped corners, central 40 percent mostly uncluttered pale tabletop reserved for a tall smartphone overlay. Let the arrangement feel candid and art-directed, not symmetrical. Leaves and soft diagonal sunlight shadows connect the edges with the center. No phone or other device in the generated image.
Lighting/mood: warm morning sunlight from upper left, gentle real shadows, fresh and optimistic, premium food magazine photography.
Color palette: ivory and soft butter yellow table, deep leaf greens, tomato red and natural citrus orange, restrained rather than neon.
Constraints: photograph only; no text, lettering, labels, UI, charts, icons, logos, packaging, watermarks, collage borders or rounded frames. No flowers. Do not depict any measured data or measurement device.
