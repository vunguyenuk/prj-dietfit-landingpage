Understand your meals.

Deep Scan

One photo. Every ingredient.

Deep Scan takes a closer look at every ingredient, even in the messiest meals. AI missed something? Add, remove, or adjust ingredients yourself to fine-tune your nutrition estimate.

  1. 1/3 Tap + and choose Scan food.
  2. 2/3 Take or pick a photo, and Deep Scan reads every ingredient.
  3. 3/3 Check each ingredient, tell the AI what it missed, and log the meal.

Barcode

Packaged food? Just scan it.

Point the camera at the barcode and Dietfit finds the product and its nutrition facts. Pick your serving and it's logged.

  1. 1/3 Tap + and choose Scan food.
  2. 2/3 Switch to Barcode and point at the pack.
  3. 3/3 Check the serving, then log it.

AI Coach

A little unsure? Ask Sara.

Your AI nutrition coach who knows more than just your name. From your daily meals and eating habits to your goals and activity, Sara uses your Dietfit history to give advice that's personal to you. The more you log, the more Sara gets you.

  1. 1/3 Tap Sara on Home.
  2. 2/3 Send a photo or ask anything.
  3. 3/3 Get an answer that knows your day, down to a meal that fits.

Meal Plan

Your goals. Your taste. Your meal plan.

Meet Dietfit's personalized Meal Plan, built around your calorie goals and food preferences. Get daily meal suggestions, swap dishes you're not craving, and tick off what you actually ate.

  1. 1/3 Open the Meal Plan tab.
  2. 2/3 Tap Create, and Dietfit builds your week from your habits.
  3. 3/3 Swap any dish for an AI suggestion.

Reminders

The right nudge, right on time.

Set your meal times once and Dietfit reminds you when it's time to log. Tap the reminder and the camera is ready.

  1. 1/3 Open Profile, then Reminders.
  2. 2/3 Set a time for each meal.
  3. 3/3 When the reminder comes, tap it to log from the camera.

A few things you might wonder

Answers about scanning, Sara and your daily tracking. Still have a question? Get in touch with us.

Are photo-based calorie estimates exact?

No. Results depend on the photo and the visible ingredients and portions. Oils, sauces and hidden ingredients can be difficult to identify. Deep Scan lets you review ingredients and edit weights before logging.

How do Quick Scan and Deep Scan differ?

Quick Scan helps you log quickly. Deep Scan goes further: it outlines foods and shows individual ingredients and their calories. You can correct names or edit ingredients before saving.

What does Sara use to answer my questions?

Your goal and onboarding answers, meals you log, and activity from the health apps you choose to connect. What you share helps Sara offer guidance with more context.

Does a meal plan automatically log my meals?

No. A meal plan is a plan. Tick or log the meal you actually ate to record it. You can also swap meals, generate a new plan and review the history.

Where can I connect activity data from?

Dietfit connects with health apps and devices you use, such as Apple Health and Apple Watch. Activity sits beside calories in so you can follow food and movement together. Availability depends on the connections you enable in the app.

Does Sara replace a medical professional?

No. Sara is an AI coach that helps you understand meals and habits. Values are estimates, and guidance does not replace medical diagnosis, treatment or advice.

Eat whatever you like. Just remember to check Dietfit!
