## Design Context

### Users
Vietnamese Gen Z and young adults who want to understand meals, calories, exercise, and progress without following an overly restrictive diet.

### Brand Personality
Warm, energetic, and approachable. Dietfit should feel encouraging and culturally relevant rather than clinical or judgmental.

### Aesthetic Direction
A bright editorial landing page led by Dietfit orange, warm neutrals, real people, large Vietnamese typography, and purposeful scroll-driven motion. Production naming and content must remain entirely Dietfit-owned.

### Design Principles
- Make nutrition feel understandable rather than intimidating.
- Use clear Vietnamese hierarchy and Be Vietnam Pro throughout.
- Let real imagery and product UI carry the story; avoid decorative clutter.
- Keep motion smooth, purposeful, and respectful of reduced-motion preferences.
- Build sections as readable, editable HTML/CSS/JS rather than runtime-injected markup.

### Reference boundaries (confirmed 20 October 2026)
- Version 1 at git 909d84a owns page visuals: white, #110b20, #FE692E, Be Vietnam Pro, existing photos, cards and footer.
- Gaia owns feature composition only: alternating text/device columns, restrained outer-edge mascot placement.
- Version 2 runner-camera-v87 owns app screen geometry and internal scroll renderers. No painted characters, parchment, orb-themed page or imported global stage.
- Preserve original hero markup and all Rive assets/runtime byte-for-byte.
- Scroll must drive the contents of the phones; timed autoplay and whole-phone tilts are not substitutes.

- Everfit supplies feature context and enlarged UI detail composition only. Use existing Dietfit imagery/state, preserve the approved layout and native UI, and reduce decorative duplicates on compact screens.
