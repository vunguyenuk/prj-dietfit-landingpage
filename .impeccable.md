## Design Context

### Users
Vietnamese Gen Z and young adults who want to understand meals, calories, exercise, and progress without following an overly restrictive diet.

### Brand Personality
Warm, energetic, and approachable. Dietfit should feel encouraging and culturally relevant rather than clinical or judgmental.

### Aesthetic Direction
A bright editorial landing page led by Dietfit orange, warm neutrals, real people, large Vietnamese typography, and purposeful scroll-driven motion. Production naming and content must remain entirely Dietfit-owned.

### Design Principles
- Make nutrition feel understandable rather than intimidating.
- Use clear Vietnamese hierarchy and Be Vietnam Pro throughout.
- Let real imagery and product UI carry the story; avoid decorative clutter.
- Keep motion smooth, purposeful, and respectful of reduced-motion preferences.
- Build sections as readable, editable HTML/CSS/JS rather than runtime-injected markup.
