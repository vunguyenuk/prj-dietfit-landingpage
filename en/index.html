Things you might want to know

How is Dietfit different from other calorie trackers?

Instead of making you log every item, Dietfit uses AI to recognize meals from a photo, estimate calories and macros, score each meal and suggest improvements. You understand what you’re eating faster, without the tedious logging.

Can Dietfit really recognize any food from a photo?

Dietfit analyzes the food and portions visible in your photo to estimate its nutrition. Clear, well-lit photos that show the whole dish give more accurate results. For harder-to-recognize dishes, you can still describe them by text or voice, or enter them manually.

Do I have to re-enter every food each day?

No. You can snap a photo, upload one you already have, or describe your meal by text or voice. Foods you eat often are saved, so logging them is even faster next time.

How does Dietfit set my weight-loss calorie goal?

Dietfit uses your body stats, BMR, BMI, TDEE, activity level and weight goal to calculate the right number of calories for each day. That gives you a concrete target to follow instead of guessing how much you should eat.

What does a meal’s health score mean?

The health score reflects food quality, the balance between nutrient groups, how complete the nutrition is and your activity for the day. At a glance, you can see what’s working in a meal and what to adjust.

Does exercise change how many calories I can eat?

Yes. You can log workouts manually or sync Apple Health to track steps, activity and Calories Out. With exercise calorie credit turned on, the calories you burn are added to your daily food goal.

What if I eat less than my goal today?

Dietfit can roll over up to 200 unused kcal to the next day. That gives you some flexibility without forcing every day to look exactly the same.

How does Dietfit help me track my weight-loss progress?

You can track your weight, BMI, body-change charts, meal history, calories and macros over time. Instead of fixating on a single up or down day, you see the long-term trend more clearly.

Can I track with friends?

Yes. Dietfit has a community feed, private groups, invite links and leaderboards so everyone can share meals, tips and progress with each other.

Can I try it before subscribing?

You can scan a meal before starting your trial, then look through the available plans before deciding to subscribe.

Eat whatever you like. Just remember to check Dietfit!
