One conversation can change the quality of your meals

By Thảo Vy

Many eating decisions don’t start with hunger. They’re tied to time, emotions, work schedules and the people around us.

Ask before you fix

Instead of cutting a food right away, ask what makes you reach for it. Maybe the real issue is not having time to prep, or a workday that ran too long.

Find solutions that fit your life

A small change that fits your routine is more useful than an ideal meal plan you can’t stick with.

The right question is often worth more than a long list of rules.

Use your meal data as the starting point for a kind conversation with yourself.
