Rebuild trust with your body through small signals

By Dietfit Team

Your body constantly sends signals about hunger, fullness, energy and stress. Those signals get drowned out when we’re too busy or chasing rigid rules.

Pause for a few seconds

Before and after a meal, try noticing your hunger, satisfaction and energy. No need for long notes; a quick check-in is enough.

Pair feelings with data

Nutrition data gives you context. Your body’s sensations give you feedback. The two complement each other.

The goal isn’t to eat perfectly, but to keep learning what works for you.

Trust is built through many small observations and adjustments, not a single decision.
