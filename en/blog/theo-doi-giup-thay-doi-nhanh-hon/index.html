Why tracking the right way helps you change faster

By Minh Anh

Our memory tends to hold on to special days and skip over small, repeated actions. Tracking fills that gap.

Trends matter more than a single number

Your weight can fluctuate because of water, salt, sleep and plenty of other factors. A trend line over a few weeks means more than one morning’s result.

Data you can act on

If progress is slow, data gives you a starting point to adjust portions, activity or sleep instead of guessing.

Tracking isn’t about grading yourself. It’s about understanding yourself.

Pick a few metrics tied directly to your goal and check them at a rhythm that works for you.
